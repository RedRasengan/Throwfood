# Throwfood

## 🐾 Snack Safari

A bright, browser-playable meadow simulator inspired by animal-feeding games.

- Move your explorer with **WASD / arrow keys**, or use the on-screen joystick on touch screens.
- Pick a snack with **1–4** or the picnic dock, then **click/tap a wild animal** to toss it.
- Match each animal’s thought bubble to tame them. Tamed animals follow you and leave coins in tips every 3 seconds.
- Spend coins at the **Camp Shop** on faster movement, longer throws, a wider catch radius, bigger tips, and lucky payouts.
- Fill the **Field Guide**, complete trail quests, and rank up as your crew grows.

Run `./start.sh` and open the served URL to play.
